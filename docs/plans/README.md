# Plans

Future work only. Nothing here describes current behaviour.

- [Roadmap](roadmap.md): delivery phases and accepted direction.
- [Backlog](backlog.md): open product and release work.
- `active/`: approved plans in progress. `proposed/`: plans awaiting approval.

Every plan carries a `Status:` line near the top. When a plan ships, move its
behaviour to [system](../system/capabilities.md), its reasoning to a
[decision](../decisions/) and its measurements to [evidence](../evidence/README.md),
then delete it.
