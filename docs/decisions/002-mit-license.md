# Decision 002: MIT license and optional README credit

Status: user-approved.

## Decision

Keep beetle-lab and the rewrite under the standard MIT license. Preserve the
existing LICENSE text and Beetlestance copyright notice. Package metadata,
container labels and distribution artifacts must identify the same license.

## Intent

Make the project publicly reusable with minimal restrictions. Retain required
copyright/license notices and request a short origin credit in derivative
repositories' READMEs. README placement is appreciated, not an additional license
condition. Commercial reuse and closed-source modifications remain permitted.

## Scope

This choice applies to beetle-lab. It does not relicense beetle-assets, third-party
code, generated media, logos or brand assets. Verify redistribution terms before
bundling those materials and preserve their applicable notices/provenance.

## Consequences

Do not add attribution, noncommercial or competing-service restrictions while
labeling the result MIT. Keep any media/brand policy separate and explicit. No
Apache-2.0 or GPL change is planned for beetle-lab.
