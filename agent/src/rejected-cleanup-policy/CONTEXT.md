# Rejected cleanup funding policy

Pure allocation arithmetic for a new16384 logical staging charge. It counts ALL
held staging, permanent replay and temporary controls; native safety counts active
future writes only, avoiding a second subtraction of retained physical occupancy.
Actual worker facts, quota, positive rejected closure and fixed SQL permit own grants.
Already-funded replay bypasses new-allocation policy in its future storage owner.
Clock rollback, unknown/stale capacity and full/lowered limits refuse new funding.
Inputs are bounded to64 active reservations; no history bodies/queue are required.
